How the forge works

From one sentence to a playable game.

  1. ┌──────────────┐
│ "a cat dodg… │
└──────┬───────┘
       ▼

    01 Read

    Breaks your prompt into words and spots the action, the hero, and what to grab or avoid.

  2.   dodge  ◀──
  runner
  snake
  flappy …

    02 Pick a mechanic

    Matches your verbs to one of six game types, like "jump" to a runner or "fly" to a flapper.

  3.   HERO   🐱
  GRAB   ⭐
  AVOID  🥒

    03 Cast

    Turns nouns into characters. A cat becomes the hero, cucumbers become the danger.

  4.   ░░▒▒▓▓██
  space · deep
  night palette

    04 Paint

    Reads the setting (space, ocean, lava, candy…) and paints the world to match.

  5.   speed ▮▮▮▯▯
  "chill" ▼
  "insane" ▲

    05 Tune

    Words like "chill" or "insane" set the speed, and the game ramps up the longer you last.

  6.   ▶ PRESS SPACE
    OR TAP
  ███ 00128 ███

    06 Play

    The game boots in the cabinet. Remix it for a new mechanic with the same characters.
